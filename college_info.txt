## Fee Payment Process
Tuition fees can be paid online through the student portal using UPI, cards or net banking, or at the Accounts Office counter by demand draft. The last date is the 15th of the first month of each semester. A late fine of Rs 100 per day applies after the deadline. Fee receipts are available on the portal within one working day of payment. Refunds take 15 to 20 working days after approval.

## Wi-Fi Access
Campus Wi-Fi is available in all academic blocks, the library and hostels. Log in with your roll number as the username and your portal password. If you forget the password, use the reset link on the portal or visit IT Support with your ID card. Each student can connect up to 2 devices at the same time.

## Library Rules
Undergraduate students can borrow 4 books for 14 days, and postgraduate students can borrow 6 books for 21 days. Books can be renewed twice online. The late fine is Rs 2 per day per book. Lost books must be replaced or paid for at the current price. Silence must be maintained in the reading rooms.

## Hostel Rules
Hostel gates close at 9:30 PM. Late entry needs prior written permission from the warden. Visitors are allowed only in the visitor lounge between 4 PM and 6 PM on weekends. Room change requests must be submitted to the Hostel Office in the first two weeks of a semester. Electrical appliances such as heaters and induction stoves are not permitted.

## Bus Routes and Timings
College buses operate on 6 routes covering the city. Morning buses reach campus by 8:45 AM and evening buses leave at 4:45 PM from the main gate. A bus pass costs Rs 6,000 per semester and is issued by the Transport Office with a passport size photo and the fee receipt. Route details are displayed on the notice board near the main gate.

## ID Card Replacement
A lost or damaged ID card can be replaced at the Administration Office. Submit an application with a passport size photo, and pay Rs 150. For a lost card, a copy of the police complaint or a written declaration is required. The new card is ready within 3 working days.

## Anti-Ragging and Safety
Ragging is strictly prohibited and is a punishable offence. Students can report incidents to the Security Office or the Anti-Ragging Committee. Complaints can be made in person or by email, and the identity of the complainant is kept confidential. In an emergency, call the Security Office round the clock.

## Grievance Process
Any issue not solved by the concerned department within 7 working days can be escalated to the Administration Office. Raise a ticket with the help desk and quote the ticket number. Urgent matters involving safety or health are treated with the highest priority and are usually acknowledged within 2 hours.
